# 0.6.1 | 2026-10-11 | Sound, particles and feel fixes
- New soundtrack closer to the original's Celtic-folk-meets-rock style: all 9 tracks rewritten with synthesised distorted and acoustic guitars, picked bass, fiddle, tin whistle, piano, spiccato strings and a full drum kit (Oubliette, Promenade, Ossuary, a 6/8 jig for the Stilt Village, Clock Lung, passage, boss, menu, death)
- Combat sounds rebuilt: swings with a metallic edge; hits layered from a low thump, crunch and a wet splat; crits ring; blocks clang; parries sing; new kills, hurts, jumps, landings, rolls, gold, cells, bows and explosions
- New sounds for secret walls, runes, curses, the timed door chime, mutations, vines, malaise, and for catching fire, freezing, poison and bleeding
- New particles: speed lines and rings on crits and kills, explosion smoke and debris, flames on burning enemies, poison bubbles, blood and oil drips, frost crystals, shock sparks, ground-pound rings, a double-jump ring, red lines when hurt
- Dropped gear glows and sparkles in its quality colour (legendaries shed gold motes); gold and cells leave a glittering trail as they fly to you
- Area ambience: drips in the Oubliette and passages, fireflies in the Promenade and Stilt Village, spores in the Ossuary and Toxic Sewers, drifting ash in the Clock Lung and Ramparts, stardust in the Observatory
- Fix: zombie attacks looked like dropped frames - the swing now moves over several frames and the wind-up trembles instead of freezing; getting hit or blocking no longer freezes the frame, parries keep a short one
- Fix: tapped jumps were too short to climb shafts; a tap now clears a 3-tile step
- Fix: shots parried back by the shield could be bounced back again by monks

# 0.6 | 2026-10-10 | The original's systems
- Three-colour scrolls: Brutality (red) / Tactics (purple) / Survival (green), pick one of two; every weapon has a colour and scales with that level; Survival gives the most health
- Item quality: normal, +, ++ and Legendary, with 21 random affixes (bleed, oil, ignite, freeze, crit damage, lifesteal, extra projectile, kill bursts...); the item card shows quality colour and affixes
- Amulet slot: 4 new amulets with max health, all damage, gold, recoverable health, move speed and flask affixes
- Backpack: unlock it at the Collector to carry a spare weapon; press C to swap it with your primary
- Recoverable health: a hit leaves an orange part of the bar; hit enemies within a few seconds to win it back
- New statuses: Bleed (stacking damage over time), Oil (fire makes it explode onto neighbours), Root
- The Mutator (passages): 16 mutations, 3 slots, one pick per passage; some are unlocked with cells at the Collector
- The Blacksmith (passages): spend gold to raise an item's quality with a new affix, or reroll its affixes
- The Tailor (start): 10 outfits recolouring the body and head flame; some unlocked by Boss Cells, the Daily Challenge, the Boss Rush and the true ending
- New at the Collector: forge level (better drops), backpack, gold retention (keep some gold through death), mutation unlocks
- Timed doors: vaults near the exit that only open if you arrive in time - gold, cells, high-quality gear and a scroll inside
- Cursed chests: better loot, but any hit is fatal until you kill 10 enemies
- Runes: Vine (grow climbable vines), Ram (slam through ram floors), Spider (wall slide and wall jump), carried by rune guardians in the Promenade, Ossuary and Stilt Village
- Hidden rooms: every area has cracked walls (any attack) and ram floors (Ram rune) hiding a chest and a scroll
- Branching routes: a second, high door in the passage leads to the Toxic Sewers (Vine rune, instead of the Promenade) and the Sunset Ramparts (Spider rune, instead of the Stilt Village)
- Boss Cells go up to 5; with 2 or more the Observatory opens past the Time Keeper and the run is won only by beating the Collector (true ending)
- Malaise: at 4+ Boss Cells, the longer you stay in an area the more damage you take, and at full it drains your health; the flask pushes it back
- New modes: Daily Challenge (the same levels and weapons for everyone that day, best time recorded), Custom mode, Boss Rush
- Assist mode: adjustable damage taken and one revive per area (no achievements while on)
- 11 new achievements; new models: Mutator, Blacksmith, Tailor, timed door, curse chains, 4 amulets, 3 rune tablets, cracked wall, ram floor, vines
