# 0.4.1 | 2026-10-10 | Update log
- New "Update log" entry in the main menu and the pause menu, listing every version's changes
- The current version is shown in the bottom-right corner of the main menu

# 0.4 | 2026-10-10 | Double jump and sound
- Double jump: press jump again in mid-air for a forward flip; air attack recovery can cancel into it
- Sound system: 80 synthesized effects for footsteps, jumps, rolls, swings, hits, crits, enemy warnings, pickups, chests, teleports and menus
- 8 looping music tracks: main menu, five areas, the sluice passage and the boss fight, plus a death theme
- 6 ambience beds: dripping water, wind, the ossuary drone, waves and rain, clockwork ticking, the passage stream
- Boss music starts behind the boss gate and hands back to the area music after the win
- Separate music and sound effect volume in Options; music ducks while paused

# 0.3 | 2026-10-10 | Six new weapons
- New weapons: Bell-Breaker, Pendulum Rapier, Tide Scythe, Chain Flail, Gravedigger's Shovel and Fishbone Crossbow, each with its own model, moveset and pixel icon
- New crit rules: within 1.5 s after a roll; targets below 35% health
- The camera sits further back, showing about 30% more of the level
- Longer enemy warnings (!): melee enemies hold their wind-up, fishers crouch before leaping, enemies hesitate when they first spot you
- Hold the attack button to keep attacking; bows keep firing too
- A newly picked-up weapon is ready at once instead of inheriting the old cooldown

# 0.2.1 | 2026-10-10 | Level and stability fixes
- Removed one-way platforms that could trap you in pits
- Jump height raised to 3.7 m so three-tile steps are reliable
- Shaft platforms are evenly spaced and no longer stack on the floor
- Fixed the frozen screen after choosing Retry on the death screen
- The prologue plays on the first run or a menu New Game only, never on Retry
- "Back to nearest teleporter" in the pause menu gets you out if you are stuck

# 0.2 | 2026-10-10 | The full game
- Main menu: Continue, New Game, Achievements, Options, Cheats, Quit; switch between Chinese and English at any time
- Five areas: the Oubliette, the Promenade, the Ossuary, the Drowned-Bell Stilt Village and the Clockmaker's Lung, linked by sluice passages
- Two bosses: the Royal Guardian; the Time Keeper (three phases and "Rewind Sixty Seconds")
- Levels assembled from 46 room templates; a fog-of-war map and teleport statues
- Difficulty: Easy / Normal / Hard, with Boss Cells unlocked by winning
- Saves, the merchant, the Collector and blueprints, 21 achievements, a cheat menu (type CHURN in the main menu)
- Story: prologue, area introductions, 13 broken tablets, NPC and boss lines, an ending
- Gear: cleaver, rusty sword, broadsword, tide spear, twin daggers, frontline shield, spiked bow and three skills

# 0.1 | 2026-10-10 | Vertical slice
- The Beheaded: modelled, rigged and animated (11 clips) by Blender scripts
- Toon shading and pixelation with pixel-grid camera snapping for smooth scrolling
- Combat feel: hit-stop, screen shake, squash and stretch, slash trails and sparks
- Three-hit combos, dodge roll with invincibility, ground slam, automatic ledge climb
- The first enemies and a playable dungeon
- A test bot that pathfinds, fights, captures frames and logs frame rates
